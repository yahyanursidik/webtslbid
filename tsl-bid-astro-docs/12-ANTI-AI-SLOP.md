# 12 — Anti AI Slop

## Objective
Hasil akhir tidak boleh tampak seperti template AI.

## Red Flags
- 3–4 card dalam setiap section,
- gradient blob,
- floating card,
- giant pill,
- icon dalam bubble di semua tempat,
- “Trusted by thousands” tanpa data,
- fake stats,
- fake testimonials,
- random dashboard mockup,
- duplicate visual rhythm,
- excessive animation,
- unnecessary glassmorphism.

## Islamic AI Slop Red Flags
- arch,
- crescent/star,
- lantern,
- fake Quran,
- fake kitab,
- invented Arabic letters,
- generic mosque silhouette,
- AI-generated scholar portrait,
- random geometric Islamic pattern.

## Hallmark Rule
Setelah halaman utama selesai:
1. jalankan audit visual,
2. identifikasi pattern generik,
3. redesign bagian yang terasa template,
4. review mobile separately.
