# 10 — Design Direction

## Visual Identity
**Fresh Islamic Learning**

Makna:
- fresh,
- bersih,
- institusional,
- edukatif,
- tenang.

Bukan Arabian decorative style.

## Design Language
Editorial × Educational × Institutional

## Recommended Visual Elements
- strong typography,
- large whitespace,
- editorial grids,
- thin dividers,
- real documentation photography,
- curriculum diagrams,
- timeline,
- learning pathway.

## Avoid
- arch,
- decorative mosque imagery,
- AI Muslim characters,
- fake books,
- fake Quran,
- ornament-heavy sections,
- green/gold saturation,
- SaaS UI.
